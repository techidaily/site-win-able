### Fix 1\. Check your system requirement

 Make sure your PC meets the minimum system requirements, that’s the basic requirement.

| OS:         | Windows 2000, XP, Vista, 8, or 10                                            |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Processor:  | Intel Pentium 4 1.5 GHz or Athlon XP 1500+                                   |
| Memory:     | 256 MB                                                                       |
| Graphics:   | 64MB 3d Graphics card with Vertex Shader and Pixel Shader (VS/PS) Capability |
| DirectX:    | Version 9.0c                                                                 |
| Storage:    | 4.3 GB                                                                       |
| Sound Card: | DirectX 9.0c compatible Sound Card                                           |

1. Launch Discord.
2. Click the**gear button** on the bottom to open User settings.
