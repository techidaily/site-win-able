##  How to Unhide Specific Rows in Excel

 To reveal only specific rows while keeping all other hidden items invisible, use the following method.

 To unhide a specific row, click [the header of the row](https://fox-http.techidaily.com/new-decoding-the-best-android-photography-software-is-picku-king-for-2024/) that's above your hidden row. For instance, to unhide row

        `6`
    
 , click the header for row

        `5`
    
 .

![Select the row that's above the hidden row.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/4-select-row-above-hidden-row.png) 

 Now, press and hold down the Shift key on your keyboard and click the header of the row that's beneath your hidden row. In the above example, you'll click the header for row

        `7`
    
 (while the Shift key is held down).

 You hold down Shift and click a row header because doing so selects all the rows between your clicked items. This selects your hidden content as well, which you'll unhide in the following step.

![Hold down Shift and select the row that's beneath the hidden row.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/5-select-row-below-hidden-row.png)
